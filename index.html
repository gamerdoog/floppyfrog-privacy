FloppyFrog collects no personal data whatsoever. It has no internet access, so it could not transmit your information even if it wanted to.

This is not a promise about how we intend to behave. It is a property of how the app is built, and section 3 lists the specifics you can check for yourself.

What the app collects

Nothing. FloppyFrog does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal or identifying information. There is no account to create, no sign-in, no email address, no name, no location, no contacts, no photos, and no device or advertising identifier.

What the app stores on your device

One number: your best score. It is a single integer written to the app's private storage area on your phone, where only FloppyFrog can read it. It is never transmitted anywhere, and it is not linked to you or to your device in any way.

That is the complete list of everything FloppyFrog saves.

How you can verify this

These are structural facts about the published build, not policy statements. Each can be confirmed by inspecting the app bundle.
PropertyIn FloppyFrogConsequence
Android permissions none requested No access to camera, microphone, location, storage, or contacts.
Internet permission not held Android blocks the app from opening any network connection at all.
Third-party SDKs none No analytics, no crash reporting, no attribution libraries.
Advertising none No ad network, so no ad profiling or tracking identifiers.
In-app purchases none No billing, so no payment details are ever handled.

The absent internet permission is the load-bearing one. On Android an app without it cannot reach the network, so no amount of code inside FloppyFrog could send your data somewhere, by accident or otherwise.

Children

FloppyFrog is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included. Because nothing is gathered, there is nothing that could identify or profile a child, and no parental consent mechanism is required.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling FloppyFrog removes your saved best score along with the app. Since that score is the only thing stored and it never leaves your phone, uninstalling deletes everything the app has ever held. There is no server-side copy to request the deletion of, because there is no server.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of FloppyFrog ever collects anything, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the effective date above will change. Any such update would be visible at this same address.
